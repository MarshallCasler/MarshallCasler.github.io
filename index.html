🎬 Arthur & the Invisibles Blog 🎬

My micro-blog about the tiny-people movies. NEW ENTRIES SOON!!

Entry #3 | Mood: hyped | Rating: ★★★★☆

Arthur 3: The War of the Two Worlds (2010)

Okay so this one wraps up the whole trilogy. The big showdown between the tiny world and the regular-sized world!! Bigger stakes, bigger bugs, and Maltazard is as scary as ever. Would watch again.

Entry #2 | Mood: suspicious | Rating: ★★★★☆

Arthur and the Revenge of Maltazard (2009)

The sequel!! Maltazard is back and he's NOT happy. Arthur has to dive back into the tiny world to stop him. I like this one because the adventure keeps going and nobody stays in the garden!

Entry #1 | Mood: amazed | Rating: ★★★★★

Arthur and the Invisibles (2006)

Where it all started!! Arthur shrinks down to the size of the Invisibles (the Minimoys) hiding in the garden and has to save their world. David Bowie voices Maltazard in the English version, which is SO cool. Best one, no contest.
